Staying safe

Plain advice for everyone who joins a tour or a social, and how to tell us when something is wrong.

Money

  • Never send money, gift cards or bank details to someone you have met online or on a tour, whatever the reason given.
  • Pay iLoveLatins only through the methods Sam gives you directly. If a message about payment looks odd, call and check before you pay.
  • If anyone asks you for money, tell Sam.

While you travel

  • Check that your passport is valid and read your government’s current travel advice for Colombia before you book.
  • Buy travel insurance that covers medical care and cancellation.
  • Leave a copy of your itinerary and hotel details with someone at home.
  • Use the hotel safe. Carry a copy of your passport and only the cash you need for the day.
  • Use registered taxis or ride apps, and ask the host if you are unsure about an area.
  • Go easy on alcohol and never leave a drink unattended.

Meeting someone

  • Meet in public places first. The socials exist for exactly this.
  • Tell the host or a friend where you are going if you make plans outside the group.
  • Everyone on a tour is an adult and is there by choice. Respect a no, in any language.

Para las mujeres

  • Participar es gratis. Nadie de iLoveLatins te pedirá dinero.
  • Conoce a las personas primero en los eventos o en lugares públicos, y cuéntale a alguien de confianza a dónde vas.
  • No compartas tu dirección ni tus datos bancarios.
  • Si alguien te hace sentir incómoda, díselo al anfitrión o escríbenos.

Report a concern

Use the contact page and choose “Report a profile or a concern”, or call or text Sam at . Every report is looked into, and profiles reported as fake or suspicious are removed.
