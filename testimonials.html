Being an average Joe at 44 with a gut, at your social I felt like Brad Pitt.

CraigBooking his second tour, 2013

Read the whole letter

Hey Sam my friend, I was just going to let you know I will be joining you again this next tour. Again, many thanks for the wonderful time I had with you and your extraordinary crew last February.

I stayed for about two weeks and could not get all my dates scheduled. Sam, there were too many good ladies and just not enough time.

Being an average Joe at 44 with a gut, in the States I don’t get second looks. Well, at your social I felt like Brad Pitt. It was ironic when you came up to me toward the end of the night and said the ladies were saying I looked like Brad Pitt. I know how far off that is, but hey, it worked for me.

One of your translators, Kathrine, was with me the whole time, and I was always kept busy as she did great with scheduling for me as I wished. She carried my camera in her bag; I was able to trust her with everything. She really went above and beyond.

One day we had an indescribable time in Santa Marta, on a picturesque beach into a remarkable sunset. I think that day alone, I fell in love more than once.

Whether you jet set around the world, or you’re stuck in midwestern nowhere like I was, make the trip to Barranquilla, Colombia with Sam. You simply have to go and see it all for yourself! You don’t even know what you don’t know!

Sam, you are truly a great man. Honest, helpful, respectful, full of integrity, with a kind and caring heart. I think this is why so many serious and quality ladies come to your events.

Sam and Barranquilla have it all. You don’t even need to pack a bag, just go with Sam and I Love Latins. Who knows? Maybe one day you will return with one of what Colombia has best to offer by your side!

Muchas gracias. Smiles, Craig

Aldo G.Writing to another man thinking of going, 2021

Read the whole letter

There is no way of knowing what each and every individual will experience, but I want to tell you that at least there are opportunities that I never dreamed imaginable. I am so mad at myself about not knowing about Colombia sooner. I am so much into Colombia that I plan to go at least four times a year.

I guess what is probably going to convince you that you need to make the trip is that as long as you don’t make it, you will never know. And that alone will always bug you like it did me. Five years went by before I got the courage to finally go.

I consider it like a vacation, but with women. If you think about the money you would have paid for a vacation through a travel agent, you would have spent the money to have gone on Sam’s event.

Sam puts a lot of effort and time into coordinating the event, all in the name of love. I am still amazed at his drive to try to please everybody. I have been on many events, but I find this city, Barranquilla, particularly receptive.

I don’t want you to think that everything is perfect in Colombia, but hope to see you on the next I Love Latins romance tour.

God bless, Aldo
