What is arranged for you

Every tour covers the same four things, so you can spend the week meeting people.

  • Your hotel

    A vetted, centrally located hotel for the full length of the tour.

  • A welcome dinner

    The first night, with the group and the host, before the socials begin.

  • Two or three socials

    Organised evenings where you meet single local women face to face.

  • A bilingual host

    An English and Spanish speaker with you at every event. You do not need Spanish.

Not included: your flight. Barranquilla’s airport code is BAQ.

Fair questions

What does a tour include?

Hotel accommodation, a welcome dinner, two to three singles socials, and a local English and Spanish speaking host for the length of the trip. Flights are booked separately.

Do I need to speak Spanish?

No. A bilingual host is at every event to translate and help the conversation along.

Why do I have to commit 30 days ahead?

A good turnout takes planning. Sam needs to know a month ahead how many men are coming.

Is it really free for women?

Yes. Women join and attend at no cost, always. There is no card requirement.
