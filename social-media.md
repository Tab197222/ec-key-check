## Wording to avoid
Facebook, Instagram and YouTube remove pages that read as selling people.
- Don't write "buy", "order", "guaranteed wife" or "mail-order bride".
- Don't describe women by looks, age or "submissive / traditional" stereotypes.
- Do write about the trip, the host, the events and meeting in person.

## Paid ads (later)
Meta and Google both require an approved application before a dating or
romance-tour business can run ads. Build up a few weeks of normal posts first,
then apply; ads run without approval get the account restricted.
