What every tour includes

  • Your hotel

    A vetted, centrally located hotel for the full length of the tour.

  • A welcome dinner

    The first night, with the group and the host, before the socials begin.

  • Two or three socials

    Organised evenings where you meet single local women face to face.

  • A bilingual host

    An English and Spanish speaker with you at every event.

Not included: your flight. Barranquilla’s airport code is BAQ. Ask Sam for the full package price for your dates before you send a deposit. The deposit is non-refundable.
