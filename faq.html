The tours

What does a tour include?

A welcome dinner, two to three singles socials, and a local English and Spanish speaking host for the length of the trip. Hotel and airfare are always paid separately.

How much does a tour cost in total?

Your first romance tour is $1,495, and repeat tours are $995. Two men together on the 2 for 1 Buddy plan pay $1,595, two tours for the price of one is also $1,595, and the special offer of three years of unlimited vacations is $2,145. Hotel and airfare are always separate. See all prices.

What is the $250 deposit, and can I get it back?

It reserves your place on a tour. The deposit is non-refundable, but if you can’t make it, you can use it on a future tour within 12 months of paying it. The rest of the tour price is due 30 days before the tour starts.

Why do I have to commit 30 days ahead?

A good turnout takes planning. Sam needs to know a month ahead how many men are coming.

Can I still join a tour inside the 30 days?

Sometimes. Online reservations close, but call or text Sam to ask about a late place.

Do I need to speak Spanish?

No. A bilingual host is at every event to translate and help the conversation along.

Can you help with a visa?

Yes. A professional US immigration attorney is available when you need a fiancé, marriage or tourist visa. Ask Sam.

Where do I fly to?

For Barranquilla tours, fly into Ernesto Cortissoz International Airport, code BAQ. For the other Colombia tours, confirm the city with Sam before you book a flight.

Joining and safety

Is it really free for women?

Yes. Women join and attend at no cost, permanently. There is no card requirement.

How do you know profiles are real?

A profile needs a real photo, name, city and an age of 18 or over. Any profile reported as fake or suspicious is removed.

How do I report someone?

Use the contact page and choose “Report a profile or a concern”, or call Sam directly. Every report is looked into.

Will anyone on the tour ask me for money?

They should not. Never send money to someone you have met through any introductions service, and tell Sam if you are asked. More on the safety page.
