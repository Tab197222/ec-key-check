From first call to first date

What happens at each stage, in order, so nothing about the trip is a surprise.

Book the flight, the rest is arranged

Your hotel, the welcome dinner, the socials and a bilingual host are waiting when you land in Barranquilla.

  1. Talk to Sam

    Call or text , or send a note from the contact page. Ask about the tour, the city and the full price for your dates. Questions cost nothing.

  2. Choose a tour

    Look at the dates and pick the week that suits you. The February tour runs just after Carnaval, when hotel and airline prices are much lower.

  3. Reserve with a $250 deposit

    The deposit holds your place and is non-refundable. Reservations close 30 days before the tour starts, because a good turnout takes planning.

  4. Book your flight

    Flights are the one thing you book yourself. For Barranquilla tours, fly into airport code BAQ.

  5. Arrive and settle in

    Your hotel is arranged. The tour opens with a welcome dinner with the group and the host.

  6. Meet in person

    Two or three organised socials through the week. The bilingual host is at every one to translate and make introductions.

  7. Keep in touch

    What happens after the tour is up to the two of you.
