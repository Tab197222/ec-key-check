From first call to first date

What happens at each stage, in order, so nothing about the trip is a surprise.

Book your flight and hotel, Sam does the rest

The welcome dinner, the singles socials and a bilingual host are waiting when you land in Barranquilla.

  1. Talk to Sam

    Call or text , or send a note from the contact page. Ask about the tour, the city and the packages. Questions cost nothing.

  2. Choose a tour

    Pick the week that suits you and your package: $1,495 for a first tour, $995 if you’ve been before. The February tour runs just after Carnaval, when hotel and airline prices are much lower.

  3. Reserve with a $250 deposit

    The deposit holds your place. It’s non-refundable, but you can use it on a future tour within 12 months. The balance is due 30 days before the tour starts, when reservations close.

  4. Book your flight and hotel

    Both are always paid separately from the tour. For Barranquilla tours, fly into airport code BAQ, and ask Sam where the group is staying.

  5. Arrive and settle in

    Check in to your hotel. The tour opens with a welcome dinner with the group and the host.

  6. Meet in person

    Two or three organised socials through the week. The bilingual host is at every one to translate and make introductions.

  7. Keep in touch

    What happens after the tour is up to the two of you.
