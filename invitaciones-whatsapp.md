English version underneath so you know what it says.

The link opens the sign-up page in Spanish. Once ilovelatins.com points at the
new site, change the link to `https://ilovelatins.com/join.html?soy=mujer`.

## Tips

- **Only invite women who would want to be on the site.** Never make a profile for someone, or use her photos without asking.
- **Approve quickly.** A woman who waits days for approval loses interest. The approval email arrives at both owner addresses.
- **Verify as many as you can.** "✓ Verificada" is what convinces men the profiles are real.
- **Twenty real, active profiles** is a good point to start promoting the site to men.
