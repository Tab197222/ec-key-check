Social media posts

Twenty ready-made posts for Instagram and Facebook. Open this page on your phone.

  1. Tap Save picture. (If nothing happens, press and hold the picture and choose “Save image”.)
  2. Tap Copy caption.
  3. In Instagram tap +, pick the picture, and paste the caption. Turn on “Share to Facebook” to post to both at once.

Post one every other day, in order. Number 2 and number 7 mention tour dates, so swap in new ones when the dates change.
